## 🚀 Featured Data Analytics Projects

### 01. End-to-End Stock Market Data Extraction & Scraping Pipeline
* **Business Case:** Manual collection of historical stock data and corporate revenue streams is inefficient and highly prone to error, preventing real-time market assessment.
* **Core Stack:** `BeautifulSoup`, `yfinance`, `Pandas`, `Plotly`, `requests`
* **The Methodology:** Developed automated extraction algorithms to pull Tesla and GameStop financial data directly from public HTML tables and financial APIs, transforming raw, unstructured strings into clean, formatted Pandas DataFrames.
* **Analytical Insights:** Identified structural discrepancies in asset performance; while Tesla's share price mirrored its aggressive long-term revenue peaks over the last two decades, GameStop displayed an inverse valuation divergence.

### 02. King County Housing Market Predictive Modeling
* **Business Case:** Real estate investors and buyers in King County, WA, lack a standardized, data-driven methodology to predict property valuations, resulting in overpayment risk.
* **Core Stack:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `NumPy`, `Scikit-Learn`, `Matplotlib`
* **The Methodology:** Engineered an exploratory data analysis pipeline across 20,000+ historical sales records. Handled missing values, treated extreme pricing outliers, and mapped feature correlations to construct a Multiple Linear Regression model.
* **Analytical Insights:** Statistical testing proved that architectural home "grade" and total interior living square footage act as the primary value drivers—each incremental grade tier correlates with a predictable, statistically significant jump in market valuation.
* **Code Sandbox:** [Explore Project Directory](./02_Real_Estate_Predictive/)

### 03. Automobile Sales Performance & Interactive Tracking
* **Business Case:** Understanding consumer purchasing behavior and marketing efficacy fluctuations across varying macroeconomic conditions (such as recession cycles) to optimize operational spending.
* **Core Stack:** `Pandas`, `NumPy`, `Matplotlib`, `Seaborn`, `Folium`
* **The Methodology:** Segmented comprehensive automobile sales data into historical recession vs. non-recession blocks. Constructed interactive visual tracking mechanisms to assess advertising expenditure returns by vehicle segment.
* **Analytical Insights:** Historical performance analysis revealed that automotive volume peaked sharply during the 1994-1995 non-recession cycle. Crucially, during deep recession periods, medium-sized family vehicles captured the resilient majority share of total advertising conversion.
